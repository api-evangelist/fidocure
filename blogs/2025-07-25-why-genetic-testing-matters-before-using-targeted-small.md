Lucas Rodrigues, DVM, MS, PhD Head of Veterinary Research at FidoCure Precision medicine has revolutionized human oncology by enabling treatments tailored to the unique genetic makeup of each tumor. Now, this same approach is being increasingly adopted in veterinary medicine, bringing a new level of sophistication to cancer care in dogs. Small molecule targeted therapies are at the forefront of this movement, offering the potential for more effective and individualized treatments.
