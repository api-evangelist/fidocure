With 6 million new cases diagnosed annually in the United States alone, cancer is the leading cause of death for dogs. Cancer is a multifactorial and complex disease. For decades, the approach to treating canine cancer has been marked by limited options and a reliance on broad-spectrum treatments that often fail to account for the unique genetic profiles of individual cases.
